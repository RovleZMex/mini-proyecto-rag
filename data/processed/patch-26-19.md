# Patch 26.19

## OVERVIEW
- Worlds is right around the corner, more "new" champs join Classic, and Arena's got fresh Augments!
- Game Updates | Riot wroadie, Riot Cashmiir, sternest | 9/22/2026
> " We're soon coming up on the Worlds patch (which is the patch after this one), so our first round of pro-focused Summoner's Rift changes are coming up here. We've identified some potential fringe picks for the tournament that could also provide some normal play variety without becoming disruptive with a nudge up in power, and knocking some individual meta outliers as well (and also Nasus). Systemically, the top lane role quest has been quite successful at freeing up top laners to skip Teleport, so we think increasing their map influence through that spell (both if you take it from the start of game or just through the quest) is a fair place to give the role some power.
- Looking for more information on all things League of Legends? Check out our community-run wiki here!
- Wrong patch notes? You can find the TFT patch notes here! "

## PATCH HIGHLIGHTS
> " Coven Janna, Coven Mel, Old God Anivia, Old God Urgot will be available September 23, 2026. "

## TEAM VOICE
> " First up, we wanted to share an update on the rollout of Team Voice, which is our planned expansion to the existing pre-made party voice chat allowing you to chat with everyone on your team. While we originally announced this as coming to North America and Oceania in Patch 26.20, after extensive testing, we've made the call to postpone this a bit to make sure we're hitting the quality bar you deserve for the experience. We don't have a target patch just yet, but we're prioritizing ironing out the kinks as efficiently as we can, since we know this is a long awaited feature we need to get right. "

### Champions

#### Fizz
> " Fizz came out back in 2011 and was finally reworked in late 2016. Though many of you remember all his buttons being in the same place, he's got a missing HP bleed on his W passive, so maybe build a Sunfire Cape or two? "

##### Passive - Nimble Fighter
- Fizz can move through units and takes reduced damage from basic attacks.

##### Q - Urchin Strike
- Fizz dashes a fixed distance through an enemy, damaging them and applying on-hit effects.

##### W - Seastone Trident
- Passive: Fizz's basic attacks deal additional damage over time, scaling with his target's missing health.
- Active: For the next few seconds, Fizz's basic attacks deal bonus damage on-hit and apply Grievous Wounds for a few seconds, reducing healing by 50%.

##### E - Playful/Trickster
- First cast: Fizz hops up on his trident, becoming untargetable. After a short delay, he will crash down, damaging and
- slowing enemies around him.
- Second cast: Fizz can hop off the trident early to dash farther but deal damage in a smaller area and doesn't slow.

##### R - Chum the Waters
- Fizz throws out bait that clings onto the first enemy champion it hits. After a short delay, a mighty shark stalks its prey, dealing tons of damage, knocking up, and slowing enemies in the area.

#### Graves
> " Welcome back, pre-rework Graves! This version from 2011-2015 is a true-to-live bot lane ADC, rewarded for long duration fights and deadly burst damage against short-range enemies. Careful though, his bonus resists take time to stack up, so assassins can still jump you in the jungle. "

##### Passive - True Grit
- While in combat, Graves gains stacking Armor and Magic Resist every second, up to a cap.

##### Q - Buckshot
- Graves fires out three bullets that damage all enemies in a line. Multiple hits on the same target deal reduced damage.

##### W - Smokescreen
- Graves launches a smoke grenade that damages, slows, and nearsights enemies in an area.

##### E - Quickdraw
- Graves dashes a short distance and gains bonus attack speed for several seconds. Basic attacks reduce Quickdraw's cooldown.

##### R - Collateral Damage
- Graves fires an explosive shell that damages enemies in a line. It explodes on the first enemy champion hit, dealing reduced damage in a cone behind them.

#### Nami
> " Nami is back in action and not much has changed since her release in 2013. For those of you coming right from the modern game, her E buff is attack-only, so the modern Nami-Karthus synergy is gone. "

##### Passive - Surging Tides
- Allies struck by Nami's abilities briefly gain bonus move speed.

##### Q - Aqua Prison
- Nami lobs a bubble that damages and stuns enemies where it lands.

##### W - Ebb and Flow
- Nami unleashes a stream of water that bounces, alternating between enemies and allies. Enemies take damage while allies are healed. Each bounce is less effective than the hit before it.

##### E - Tidecaller's Blessing
- Nami empowers an ally, causing their next three attacks to deal bonus damage and slow their target.

##### R - Tidal Wave
- Nami unleashes a massive tidal wave that damages, knocks up, and slows all enemies it crashes into.

#### Nautilus
> " Nautilus came out back in 2012 and as with Nami, his kit has stood the test of time. Remember that you can buy multiple Doran's Rings if you want your laning phase to work out, and he used to be quite the jungler back in the day. The biggest difference you'll find is that his ultimate is much less disruptive to targets hit by the traveling portion. "

##### Passive - Staggering Blow
- Nautilus's basic attacks deal bonus damage and root their enemies. Each enemy can only be Staggered once every few seconds.

##### Q - Dredge Line
- Nautilus throws out his anchor, colliding with the first enemy or piece of terrain it hits. Enemies are damaged and pulled in, while Nautilus pulls himself to his target. Hitting terrain refunds part of Dredge Line's cooldown.

##### W - Titan's Wrath
- Nautilus gains a shield. While it holds, his attacks deal damage over time to enemies in the area.

##### E - Riptide
- Nautilus slams the ground, sending out cascading shockwaves that damage and slow enemies. Enemies may be hit by multiple shockwaves, taking reduced damage for subsequent hits.

##### R - Depth Charge
- Nautilus fires a shockwave into the earth that damages and knocks up enemies in its path. When it reaches his target, it damages, knocks up, and stuns all enemies in the immediate area.
