## CHAMPIONS

#### Ambessa

> " Ashe is one of the premier marksmen in pro play at the moment, and we're seeing her output too much raw damage given her position as the highest utility marksman in the game. "

##### Base Stats
- Attack Damage per Level: 3.5 ⇒ 3

#### Cassiopeia
> " Our changes two patches ago to Cassiopeia have been pretty good directionally, with most players being proportionally less impacted from those adjustments than pros. We're taking another swing at her early resources because of how dominant she still is in early laning, making her work a little harder to create an advantage. "

##### Base Stats
- Base Health: 630 ⇒ 610
- Base Mana: 480 ⇒ 450

#### Diana

## CLASSIC
> " Are you ready for MORE new old champions?! Because we are! This time around we've got an eclectic crew who have no business forming a band together. Everyone please welcome Aatrox, Caitlyn, Irelia, Karma, and Quinn to the Fields of Justice.
- As a note, the Act II Battle Pass will be arriving with Patch 26.21 "

### Champions

#### Aatrox
> " Aatrox was just a baby, 3 patches old, when we chose to snapshot him for League Classic. He only ever had four balance changes before his rework in 2017 and subsequent rework in 2018, and we're excited to see how he'll perform again. He's an attack-focused fighter, damaging himself to gain attack speed and revive when he would be killed "

#### Caitlyn
> " Classic's version of Caitlyn was live from 2011-2015. Did you know when she first came out, some players were upset that she was too similar to Miss Fortune? "

#### Irelia
> " Welcome back the 2010-2018 version of Irelia! The original Trinity Force poster child is here to even the odds no matter how bad they look. She's been Q-max, W-max, and E-max at various points in time, so see what build order works for you! She's a fast but deceptively durable fighter who loves to be the underdog. "

#### Karma
> " Karma came out just two patches after Caitlyn and was only live for just over two years before being reworked in early 2013. Karma is arguably League's first battlemage, rewarding her for getting in the thick of it and making her stronger the more health she's missing. "

##### Q - Heavenly Wave: Karma fires out blades in a fan, err, cone in front of her, damaging all enemies it hits. If empowered
- with Mantra, it also heals all allies (herself included), scaling with their missing health. W - Spirit Bond: Karma bonds with another target, giving allies (herself included) move speed, slowing enemies, and creating a tether between the two. Any champion caught in the clothesline is hasted or slowed as if they were the initial target. Enemies who get caught in the clothesline are damaged. If empowered with Mantra, all move speed effects are doubled. E - Soul Shield: Karma shields a target. If empowered with Mantra, it also damages all enemies around her target.

#### Quinn and Valor
> " Quinn and Valor have returned, having only existed in this form for just over two years, from 2013-2015. Quinn is a ranged and evasive auto-attacker, while her ultimate calls in Valor to fight as a blistering-fast melee skirmisher instead. " Passive - Harrier: Valor periodically marks nearby enemies, whom Quinn can attack to deal bonus damage. Q - Blinding Assault: Valor flies out to damage and blind enemies in an area. W - Heightened Senses: Passively gives Quinn bonus attack and move speed whenever she triggers Harrier. Activate to briefly reveal a large area around her. E - Vault: Quinn dashes to and then vaults off an enemy, damaging, knocking back, and slowing them. Valor then marks the target with Harrier.

#### Fiora
> " Fiora is still the game's most dominant top laner even after her last set of nerfs, so we're back for one more round. We don't want to take down her explosive ultimate any further, so we're just going for a generic damage nerf. "

##### W - Riposte
- Bonus Attack Damage: 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 ⇒ 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30

#### Graves
> " Graves came out swinging, being both wildly popular and arguably the game's strongest champion. We've got some feel-good changes to his animations, especially his critical strike one, and some lessfeel-good nerfs as he desperately needs them. We're looking to preserve his bursty shotgunning and unique aspects as a tanky ADC, so that means we simply have to reduce his sustained damage. "

##### Base Stats
- Basic Attack and Critical Attack Animations: Sped up
- Base Attack Damage: 54.1 ⇒ 50
- Attack Damage per Level: 3.1 ⇒ 2.5
- Attack Speed per Level: 2.9% ⇒ 2.5%

##### R - Collateral Damage
- Cone Damage: 140 / 250 / 360 + 120% bonus AD ⇒ 140 / 230 / 320 + 100% bonus AD
- Projectile Damage: Unchanged

#### Fizz
> " Fizz came out right behind Graves as a super strong performer, but mostly as an AP assassin in mid lane. We want to preserve both his assassin and fighter playstyles, so we're skewing our nerfs toward his higher-AP builds. We're pulling in lighter versions of a couple of nerfs from 2013 and 2015, aimed at bringing his guaranteed burst down to size, since he has so much reliable damage already with Deathfire Grasp and 75% AP ratio Lich Bane. "

##### Q - Urchin Strike
- Ability Power Ratio: 60% ⇒ 40%

##### W - Seastone Trident
- Active Ability Power Ratio: 35% ⇒ 20%

#### Kennen
> " Kennen hasn't quite reached the same heights as his other ninja compatriots and we're confident he isn't performing as well as he should. AD Kennen needs substantially more help than his other builds, so we're skewing his buffs in ways to help those players out most of all "

##### W - Electrical Surge
- Passive Damage: 40 / 50 / 60 / 70 / 80% total AD ⇒ 25 / 40 / 55 / 70 / 85 + 100% bonus AD

#### Nautilus
> " Unlike his other water-themed friends, Nautilus didn't come out swinging. Sometimes that anchor just weighs him down… Ultimately, he's weak in all three of his main roles (Support, Jungle, Top) and we'd like to help those latter two most of all. So we're fixing one major bug while pulling in buffs and reverting nerfs from early in his lifetime, giving him more reason to soak up experience and wade into the thick of the fight. "

##### Passive - Staggering Blow
- Bug Fix: Fixed a bug where the empowered attack was critting for too much damage.

##### W - Titan's Wrath

- Shield Duration: 9.75 ⇒ 10
