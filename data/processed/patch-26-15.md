- Ranked Season 3 will start at 12pm on July 29 local server time. Victorious Ranked Mission (15 Ranked Games won) will be refreshed with season start, and end of S2 rewards will be distributed shortly after S3 start. Shard transfer service will be temporarily off until S2 rewards are granted.

## AEGIS OF VALOR
> " Aegis of Valor has helped make autofill games feel worth playing out, but its loss protection and Mastery requirement created a secondary objective: play for the grade, not necessarily for the win. That's the opposite of what a Ranked incentive should ask of you. We want every Ranked teammate to share the same goal, which is trying their very best to win the game. "
- We're refocusing Aegis entirely around victory: it will no longer reduce LP losses in the case of defeat, and wins will award double LP without requiring a minimum mastery score.

## APEX DUO RESTRICTIONS
> " When we brought duo queue back to Apex, our goal was to let more high-ranked players play together without compromising the quality of the ladder. The results were mixed. In Master, duos were generally manageable, but we still saw enough impact on match quality to warrant tighter limits. In GM and Challenger, smaller player pools and extremes of skill give duos an outsized impact on game outcomes, one we can't consistently offset without creating excessive skill gaps or less competitive lobbies. As our matchmaking systems improve, we'll keep looking for ways to bring duo queue back to GM and Challenger, but only once we can do so without compromising the fairness and competitive integrity they're meant to represent. "
- Master players will now be able to duo, but only with players close to them in rank.
- GM, Challenger, and players with GM or Challenger MMR will not be allowed to duo.
- For more details on the exact restrictions, see here.

#### Bel'Veth

## ARAM: MAYHEM

#### " Hello Mayhemers!
- In this patch, we're making a systematic update to champion balance modifiers. For a long time, champion damage modifiers only affected spells and attack damage by champions. This led to some extreme cases where champions could overperform because their item and Augment damage wasn't affected.
- With this update, damage dealt modifiers now apply to spell, attack, item, and Augment damage. Our goal is for these modifiers to better reflect a champion's overall performance while allowing underperforming champions to benefit more consistently from the rest of the game's systems. As a result, we're able to remove many existing champion specific modifiers.
- Over time, we hope to continue reducing the number of individual champion mods and make future balance changes more targeted and easier to maintain. For Augments, we're continuing to buff underperforming options to make their payoffs more satisfying in combat. On the other hand, Critical Missile has proven to be a bit too reliable on critical strike ADCs, so we're tuning it down to make it less frustrating to play against. Thanks for playing, and we'll see you on the Bridge! "
